How to play

  • On a phone: your player runs to the ball without your help. You play the shot with one finger, anywhere on the screen, as the ball reaches you:
    • Tap your side of the court to walk there. Your player goes there first, still runs to the ball when it comes, and returns to your spot after the shot.
    • Swipe toward where you want the ball to go. A longer swipe goes deeper.
    • Fast swipe: a fast ball.
    • Curved swipe: the ball spins and bends in the air.
    • Hold, then let go: a lob. Hold, then swipe, to aim the lob.
    • Double tap: a smash, when the ball is above your head.
    • Swipe down near the net: a soft drop shot.
    • Serve: swipe up toward where you want the serve to go. A fast swipe is a hard serve, a slow swipe is a soft one, and a curved swipe adds spin. A plain tap gives a soft safe serve.
  • Serve on a computer: left and right arrows (or a mouse click on the court) move the yellow ring. Press Space when the bar is in the green.
  • On a computer — move: arrow keys or W A S D.
  • HIT (Space): a normal shot. Press it as the ball reaches you. The ring at your feet turns green when the ball is in reach.
  • LOB (L): a high ball over their heads. Use it when both rivals stand at the net.
  • Aim: press left or right as you hit, or just after. The ball goes to that side and a yellow ring shows where it will land. Up makes it deep, down (when you are near the net) makes a soft drop shot. With no direction the ball goes to the open space.
  • Smash: when they lob, an orange SMASH ring shows where to stand and the HIT button turns red. Get under the ball and press HIT while it is above your head. If you miss it, let it bounce and play it off the glass.
  • The glass: the ball may bounce once on your side. After that bounce it can come off the glass and you can still play it.
  • Out: a ball that hits the glass on the other side before the ground is out.
  • Scoring: 15, 30, 40, game. At 40-all the next point wins (golden point).
  • Long rallies: the team that wins a rally of 10 shots or more celebrates.
  • The white ring shows where the ball will bounce.
